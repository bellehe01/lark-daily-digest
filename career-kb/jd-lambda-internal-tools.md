## Role Overview

Partners with business teams to find where manual work, inconsistent data, or missing tooling is slowing things down, and helps turn that into a shipped fix. The role exists to close the gap between what leadership needs to see and what teams can easily report: turning manual reporting into trusted, self-serve data products; finding and fixing the workflows costing teams the most time; helping make sure the highest-impact tooling requests actually get built.

## Responsibilities

**Data Products & Reporting**
- Partner with stakeholders to define key metrics and establish single trusted sources
- Support converting manual reporting into live, self-serve executive dashboards
- Prioritize automation based on current manual effort, data staleness, and error rates

**Workflow Automation**
- Identify spreadsheet-driven work suitable for automation
- Determine whether to build lightweight solutions directly or escalate to engineering
- Write clear specifications for technical partners
- Document opportunities for AI agents and automation

**Intake & Prioritization**
- Manage tool-request pipeline: collection, triage, and prioritization
- Maintain visible roadmaps for stakeholder transparency
- Build relationships with supported teams

## Qualifications

- Early-career product, business operations, analytics, or technical program management experience
- Comfort working directly with data, spreadsheets, and lightweight tooling (SQL, no-code platforms, AI coding assistants)
- Some exposure to designing a workflow or tool from the end user's point of view
- Track record of shipping deliverables (a tool, process fix, or internal script), not just recommendations

**Core competencies**
- End-user experience judgment for dashboards and tools
- Impact-versus-effort prioritization
- AI-assisted lightweight execution
- Cross-functional relationship building
- Clear written communication (specs, roadmaps, requirements)
