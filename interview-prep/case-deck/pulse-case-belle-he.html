Objectives & Baseline

Define the pond before fishing: vertical scope, baseline discipline, targets with a basis

Scope and metric conventions come first; every number later in this deck traces back to this page.
1 · Vertical definition & sizing
  • Pan-music creators: singing, instruments, DJ sets, production. Broad at the vertical level, precise per creator
  • The five managed creators are the vertical’s showcase anchors; recruitment extends to short-video musicians
  • The definition sets the size of the pond: recruitable pool estimated from tagged music content (tooling + manual tagging)
2 · Baseline discipline
  • Baseline = 7-day averages at period start and end, outlier days (platform mega-campaigns) removed
  • Success is read as campaign period vs baseline, never raw totals
  • Key campaign actions are scheduled to cover the measurement window, not drift past it
3 · Targets, with a basis
  • Vertical: daily streaming actives and daily revenue, both up vs baseline
  • Campaign top creators: revenue +100%+ during campaign, paying-user share +30–40%, per-payer spend +20–45%, follower growth
  • ROI ≈ 5–6×: campaign-period revenue ÷ $80K (convention to confirm)
Targets come from comparable-campaign history and get locked against internal baseline data in week 1. A target without a baseline convention is an opinion.

Festival Activation · Overview

One festival, three funnels: recruit the new, energize the existing, showcase the top

The plan: “From Your Room to the Main Stage” · the Road to Pulse Creator Program, in five phases. Excitement isn't a separate workstream; it's the output of the other three, clipped and amplified.
1 · Sign-up & warm-up2 · Launch3 · Online rounds4 · Festival weekend5 · Post-festival push
RECRUIT · the Rising division of "Road to Pulse": a dedicated on-ramp for musicians who have never gone LIVE
ENERGIZE · existing creators compete in the Headliner division; 4-week bootcamp + archetype coaching
SHOWCASE · TikTok LIVE stage at Pulse: finals + top-talent performances, all streamed
The budget is deliberately concentrated: the stage and the competition compound each other, and marketing amplifies a story the competition creates for free.

Festival Activation · Recruitment & Education

"Road to Pulse", modeled on On Deck: Breakaway, with the entry gate the official campaigns don't have

Proven architecture, deliberately copied. My changes target what On Deck leaves open: recruiting pre-LIVE musicians, and day-31 aftercare.

“The Final Drop”: top 3 perform on stage; winner revealed live on air, their track becomes a limited Gift that night
The reward ladder (On Deck's design: career capital over cash)
WhoRewardCost

Even On Deck's cash-equivalent prizes are modest: the top of the ladder isn't bought, it's platform access. That's why $18K is enough.
Inside every entrant's room (On Deck's production mechanics)
  • Campaign-exclusive Pulse Gift with in-room counter
  • Milestone progress bar: gift-point thresholds unlock the next reward tier
  • Live rank badge shown in the room; leaderboard resets weekly
  • Shout-out segment: entrants thank top supporters on air, a built-in retention ritual
I watched these mechanics run live in On Deck rooms; they double as coaching material for the portfolio.
Runs alongside: education and the pipeline. A 4-week bootcamp covers streaming craft and the 1K → Gifts → Subscriptions → Rewards ladder, with portfolio creators as mentors. After the festival, every entrant enters a 30-day incubation track (first LIVE within 2 weeks, 3 streams/week by day 30). The festival is a pipeline, not a finish line. And On Deck itself runs different missions per tier; the platform already coaches by archetype, and I apply the same logic inside the portfolio.

Festival Activation · Ignition & On-site

Heat is engineered: seed it, spike it, then harvest it on site

On TikTok, hot lists run on derivative clips and planted moments. Nothing ignites from a cold start.
Making it trend
  • Anthem co-creation: a star artist records the festival anthem in warm-up; creators cover it, fans duet it
  • Planted show moments: engineered contrast pairings (A×B co-streams) turned into events, pushed with trending-topic words in 2-day concentrated bursts
  • Top creators post through the campaign: participation ≥80% is a tracked target; amplifier accounts scale the clips
Creator × fan second creation
  • Fan clip missions: second-creation submissions ranked by like tiers, with reward ladders
  • Targeted, not broadcast: offered 1v1 to high-stickiness, mass-fan creators; payer structure decides who runs fan campaigns
  • Open clip rights + attendee UGC: booth QR prompts festival-goers to post; best fan clips hit the stage screens
On-site & partners
  • Sponsorship covers non-content site costs; stage & show programming curated from competition winners; creator-level QR attribution on stage and booth
  • Program team is measured on qualified creators + platform heat; we deliver pre-event posting in return
  • BD team assists recruitment; product team ships custom event effects; labels clear licensing from W1; VIP-ticket inventory and booth placement locked in the SLA
