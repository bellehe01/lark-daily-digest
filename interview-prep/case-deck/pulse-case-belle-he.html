Creator Strategy

Grow two, fix two, sustain one: prioritized by where coaching changes the trajectory

Each archetype gets a different playbook. Coaching effort is a scarce resource; it goes where it bends the curve.
Grow
Echo Music (first priority): momentum is perishable. Fixed schedule, fan club & subscriptions push, welcome rituals, convert surge viewers into followers.
Luna Live: codify her playbook for the whole portfolio; scale hours gently (58 → ~75).
Fix
Vinyl Nights: fewer, better hours (126 → ~80); format refresh; co-host slots with rising creators.
DJ Nova: content refresh, off-peak experiments, festival-stage exposure as a new-audience injection.
Sustain
BeatTheory: prevention, not rescue. Runs Luna’s codified playbook, one new-format experiment per month; alert if returning % keeps rising while growth falls (early discovery-starved pattern).
Coaching all five equally: rejected. Echo comes first because momentum decays fastest; a surge unconverted this month is gone next month.
Festival roles are part of the fix: Echo & Luna showcase on the LIVE stage · Nova gets a stage slot as a new-audience injection · Vinyl co-hosts the semifinal events · BeatTheory mentors the bootcamp.
Additional data I would request
  • Traffic source split: For You feed vs. followers (separates the two archetypes cleanly)
  • New vs. returning viewers in absolute counts, and viewer → follower conversion
  • Gifting concentration: how much revenue depends on top gifters
  • Subscription penetration, time-slot performance, 90-day trend (30 days can mislead)
How I measure success
  • Portfolio revenue per LIVE hour: the efficiency north star
  • New-viewer share ↑ for Nova & Vinyl (discovery fix working)
  • Returning-viewer rate ↑ for Echo (retention fix working)
  • Portfolio MoM revenue positive within 60 days (lagging validation)

Excitement isn't a separate workstream; it's the output of the other three, clipped and amplified.

Festival Activation · Recruitment & Education

"Road to Pulse", modeled on On Deck: Breakaway, with the entry gate the official campaigns don't have

Proven architecture, deliberately copied. My changes target what On Deck leaves open: recruiting pre-LIVE musicians, and day-31 aftercare.
Open entry · W2
two divisions, Rising and Headliner (On Deck's structure): newcomers never compete against established streamers
→
Weekly rounds · W3–5
multi-signal points: LIVE hours, campaign gifts, short-video engagement (per On Deck missions)
→
Semifinals · W6
special judges review original sets; co-hosted duels via In Sync matching
→
Finals at Pulse · W8
top 3 perform on the TikTok LIVE stage
The reward ladder (On Deck's design: career capital over cash)
WhoRewardCost
All entrantsPulse avatar frame + campaign badge≈ $0, platform asset
Round winnersDiamonds + festival merchlow
SemifinalistsVIP Pulse tickets, travel coveredthe bulk of $18K
Top 3Stage slot + distribution support (as in Music On Stage) + own track released as a limited-run LIVE Gift (as in Songs of LIVE)≈ $0, priceless
Even On Deck's cash-equivalent prizes are modest: the top of the ladder isn't bought, it's platform access. That's why $18K is enough.
Inside every entrant's room (On Deck's production mechanics)
  • Campaign-exclusive Pulse Gift with in-room counter
  • Milestone progress bar: gift-point thresholds unlock the next reward tier
  • Live rank badge shown in the room; leaderboard resets weekly
  • Shout-out segment: entrants thank top supporters on air, a built-in retention ritual
I watched these mechanics run live in On Deck rooms; they double as coaching material for the portfolio.
Runs alongside: education and the pipeline. A 4-week bootcamp covers streaming craft and the 1K → Gifts → Subscriptions → Rewards ladder, with portfolio creators as mentors. After the festival, every entrant enters a 30-day incubation track (first LIVE within 2 weeks, 3 streams/week by day 30). The festival is a pipeline, not a finish line. And On Deck itself runs different missions per tier; the platform already coaches by archetype, and I apply the same logic inside the portfolio.

Festival Activation · On-site, Marketing & Partners

On site, the festival is both a stage and a content factory

Three days of festival → eight weeks of content and three partner relationships that outlive the event.
On-site activations
  • TikTok LIVE stage: competition finals + portfolio top-talent showcases, all streamed to the platform
  • Backstage co-streams with festival artists (cleared in advance with labels)
  • Discovery booth: attendees start their first LIVE on-site with staff guidance; recruitment made physical
  • QR attribution: stage and booth QR codes deep-link to each creator's LIVE, so festival impact is measurable per creator
Marketing & community
  • Co-branded hashtag challenge with Pulse, seeded by competition entrants
  • Weekly competition highlights clipped to short-form; short-form reach feeds LIVE viewership
  • Entrant community spaces: peer support now, retention channel after the festival
External partner management
  • Festival org: deliverables SLA + weekly sync; stage specs locked by week 6
  • Agencies / Creator Networks: recruitment channels with entry targets per partner
  • Labels & artists: co-stream clearances start week 1: licensing lead time is the long pole
