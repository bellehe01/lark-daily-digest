Objectives & Baseline

Scope, baseline, targets: in that order

Vertical definition, measurement conventions and targets, set before any action starts.
1 · Vertical definition & sizing
  • Pan-music creators: singing, instruments, DJ sets, production. Broad at the vertical level, precise per creator
  • The five managed creators are the showcase anchors; recruitment extends to short-video musicians
  • Persona: 16–34, urban, EDM / DJ-culture adjacent; DJ sets, remixing, singer-songwriters, instrumentalists — validate wk 1
  • Illustrative sizing: tens of thousands of tagged music creators in market, roughly a quarter not yet LIVE; tooling + manual tagging refine this in week 1
2 · Baseline discipline
  • Baseline = 7-day averages at period start and end, outlier days (platform mega-campaigns) removed
  • Success is read as campaign period vs baseline, never raw totals
  • Key campaign actions are scheduled inside the measurement window
3 · Targets, with a basis
  • Vertical: daily streaming actives +8–10%, daily revenue +12–15% vs baseline (illustrative); actives carried by tail and new creators, revenue by the top
  • Campaign top creators: revenue +100%+ during campaign, paying-user share +30–40%, per-payer spend +20–45%, follower growth
  • ROI ≈ 5–6×: campaign-period revenue ÷ $80K (convention to confirm)
  • Basis: comparable-campaign history; locked against internal baseline data in week 1

On Deck itself runs different missions per tier; the platform already coaches by archetype, and I apply the same logic inside the portfolio.

Festival Activation · Ignition & On-site

Ignition: trending words, planted moments, fan second-creation

On TikTok, hot lists run on derivative clips and planted moments. Nothing ignites from a cold start.
Making it trend
  • Anthem co-creation: a star artist records the festival anthem in warm-up; creators cover it, fans duet it. Every trending word and challenge hangs off the program’s big idea
  • Planted show moments: engineered contrast pairings (A×B co-streams) turned into events, pushed with trending-topic words in 2-day concentrated bursts
  • Top creators post through the campaign: participation ≥80% is a tracked target; amplifier accounts scale the clips
Creator × fan second creation
  • Fan clip missions: second-creation submissions ranked by like tiers, with reward ladders
  • Invited 1v1: offered to high-stickiness, mass-fan creators; payer structure decides who runs fan campaigns
  • Open clip rights + attendee UGC: booth QR prompts festival-goers to post; best fan clips hit the stage screens
On-site & partners
  • Sponsorship covers non-content site costs; stage & show programming curated from competition winners; creator-level QR attribution on stage and booth
  • Program team is measured on qualified creators + platform heat; we deliver pre-event posting in return
  • BD team assists recruitment; product team ships custom event effects; labels clear licensing from W1; VIP-ticket inventory and booth placement locked in the SLA
