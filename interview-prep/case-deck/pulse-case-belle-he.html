Festival Activation · Architecture

One festival, three funnels: recruit the new, energize the existing, showcase the top

The plan: “From Your Room to the Main Stage” · the Road to Pulse Creator Program, in five phases.
Of the many possible plays, this program concentrates on two mechanisms not yet tried in this vertical but proven elsewhere: a creator×fan second-creation loop, and event marketing that amplifies showcases.
1 · Sign-up & warm-up · W1–22 · Launch · W23 · Online rounds · W3–64 · Festival weekend · W7–85 · Post-festival push · W8+detail: p.8
Recruit
The Rising division of Road to Pulse: a dedicated on-ramp for musicians who have never gone LIVE. Short-video missions count as entry, so the funnel starts one level below the official campaigns’ eligibility gates.
Feeds: daily streaming actives, new-creator supply
Energize
Existing creators compete in the Headliner division; a 4-week bootcamp covers streaming craft and the monetization ladder; portfolio creators mentor and perform, which doubles as their discovery fix.
Feeds: engagement, revenue, portfolio recovery
Showcase
The TikTok LIVE stage at Pulse: competition finals and top-talent performances, all streamed to the platform. The stream is the product for the 99% who are not on site.
Feeds: platform heat, brand, showcase goal

The top of the ladder is platform access, not cash. Mirrors On Deck: Breakaway and Music On Stage.
Inside every entrant’s room
  • Campaign-exclusive Pulse Gift with in-room counter; milestone progress bar unlocks reward tiers
  • Live rank badge; leaderboard resets weekly
  • Shout-out segment: entrants thank top supporters on air, a built-in retention ritual
Creator×fan second creation
  • Fan clip missions ranked by like tiers; open clip rights so fans can cut freely
  • Offered 1v1 to high-stickiness, mass-fan creators; payer structure decides who runs fan campaigns
  • Attendee UGC collected on site via booth QR
Amplification & spread
  • Topic planning and pre-planned collaborative moments run through a partner amplification network
  • Top creators post through the campaign; ≥80% participation target
These mechanics are new to this vertical (proven in comparable ecosystems); sizing and payout impact will be measured against internal data after onboarding.

Festival Activation · On-Site Delivery, Partners & Risk

On site, the festival is a stage and a content factory

Three days of festival, eight weeks of content, and partner relationships that outlive the event.
On-site delivery
  • Sponsorship covers non-content site costs; stage & show programming curated from competition winners
  • Discovery booth: attendees start their first LIVE with staff guidance
  • QR attribution: stage and booth codes deep-link to each creator’s LIVE, so impact is measurable per creator
  • On-site clipping desk: same-day highlight clips feed the hashtag while the festival is still on
Partner management
  • Festival org: deliverables SLA + weekly sync; stage specs, VIP-ticket inventory and booth placement locked by W6
  • Program team: measured on qualified creators + platform heat; we deliver pre-event posting in return
  • BD team assists recruitment; product team ships custom event effects; labels clear licensing from W1
Core plan vs stretch plan · separated by resource assumptions
Core (committed)Runs on confirmed resources only: competition + finale on a licensed catalog cleared in W1; stage and booth per the signed SLA; ≥90% sessions delivered; zero material rights or safety incidents
Stretch (if resources land)Star anthem collaboration and artist co-streams (artist & label approval); custom event effects (product bandwidth); a breakout showcase push toward a 5,000+ concurrent-viewer moment
