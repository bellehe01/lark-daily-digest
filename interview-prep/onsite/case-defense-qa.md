## 开场 · 怎么介绍这个作业(必背,约 75 秒)

**框架**(老师定调):这是一份 **30 天融入计划**,活动只是其中一块。融入 → 诊断 → 项目 → 度量。

**中文要点**
- 定位先说对:题目问的是"你上任第一个月做什么",所以这是**融入计划 + 项目推进节奏 + 活动方案**三层,不是一个活动策划案
- 融入:第一周对齐基线口径、和五位创作者 1:1、和各协作方对齐(立项会)
- 诊断:效率问题不是努力问题——播最多的两人跌得最狠
- 项目:音乐节当增长引擎,招新/修存量/秀头部三个漏斗,五阶段推进 + DRI + 决策点
- 度量:所有数字是假设,W1 对内部数据校准;Day 30 有明确交付

**English**

> "The brief asks what I'd do in my first month, so the deck is built as an integration plan, not an event proposal — three layers. First, integration: week one is baseline alignment, 1:1s with all five creators, and a kickoff that locks owners and decision rules with every partner team. Second, the diagnosis: the data shows an efficiency problem, not an effort problem — the two creators streaming the most hours are declining fastest — so each creator gets one verdict and one plan. Third, the program: the festival becomes the growth engine — Road to Pulse recruits new musicians, re-energizes the existing ones, and showcases the top, run in five phases with owners and decision points. And everything is measured: every target is a hypothesis, recalibrated against internal data in week one, with a concrete day-30 deliverable. Happy to go deeper anywhere."

## 通用技巧 · 面试官给补充信息时,怎么确认口径(老师教的)

> 不要直接问"这个创作者是成熟期还是成长期?"——那是你的运营内部动作,直接问显得新手。
> 要问**能帮你判断的背景信息**,自己下判断:
>
> **English:** *"Before I answer — is there context on how these creators were classified? For example, is 'top creator' here defined by stable revenue over the last 30 to 60 days, or a recent spike?"*
>
> 对方说"稳定" → "Then my analysis holds as-is." 对方说"最近窜上来的" → "Then I'd treat them as growth-stage, not established — the priority shifts to stabilizing the performance before scaling it."

## A · 设计思路与元问题

### A1. 你认为核心问题是什么?为什么这样定义?

**考点**:问题定义能力。
**框架**:证据 → 定义 → 定义决定动作。

**中文要点**
- 表面:组合月环比 −3.4%。真正的问题:努力和产出脱钩
- 证据:Nova 115 小时、Vinyl 126 小时,是播得最多的两个人,却跌 −18%/−20%;Echo 38 小时,$318/小时,+22%
- 每小时营收差 5 倍($62–$318)
- 定义成"效率问题"决定了动作是修结构(更好的时长、拉新观众),不是让大家播更多

**English**

> "The portfolio is down 3.4% month over month, but that's the symptom. The real problem is that effort and output have decoupled: the two creators streaming the most hours — 115 and 126 — are declining fastest, while Echo streams 38 hours at $318 per hour and grows 22%. Revenue per hour spans five x across five creators. I framed it as an efficiency problem because the framing dictates the action: if it were an effort problem you'd push more hours, and the data says more hours is exactly what's failing. So the plan restructures hours and fixes audience flow instead."

### A2. 你怎么排除大盘/市场因素?"你怎么知道不是整个市场在跌?"

**考点**:归因能力。老师点名:baseline 剔了大促,但没提行业对照。
**框架**:内部分化证据 → 基线纪律 → 内部对照组。

**中文要点**
- 最直接的证据:五个人同期分化——两人涨(+22%/+9%)、三人跌。大盘下行会同向压所有人,不会精确地压中播得最多的两个
- Baseline 纪律:7 天均值、剔除平台大促
- 入职后第一周:用**非音乐垂类做内部对照组**,对齐同期趋势;如果全平台都在跌,目标随对照组校准

**English**

> "Three checks. First, the pattern itself: if the market were falling, all five would fall together. Instead two are growing over twenty and nine percent while three decline — and the decline concentrates exactly on the highest-hour creators. A macro trend doesn't select victims by streaming hours. Second, the baseline discipline in the deck: seven-day averages with platform mega-campaign days removed. Third — and this needs internal data — in week one I'd set up a control group from non-music verticals and read every trend against it. If the whole platform is down, targets get recalibrated against that control."

### A3. 你的核心假设是什么?如果错了,方案怎么变?

**考点**:假设意识 + 弹性。guideline 原题。
**框架**:四个假设,每个带"错了怎么办"。

**中文要点**
1. **30 天快照能代表趋势** → 错了:W1 拉 90 天数据重看,诊断可能翻,所以每个人的方案都在 W1 1:1 里验证后才执行
2. **行业基准适用于这个垂类**(ROI 5×、+30–40% 等) → 错了:数字全部重校准,机制不变,W4 检查点会暴露偏差
3. **开放报名能拉到 500** → 错了:20–30% 超额外联 + 机构预承诺 + W4 触发付费投放
4. **二创机制可以迁移进这个垂类** → 错了:有 Plan B(见 D1),而且它不在关键路径上

**English**

> "Four assumptions, each with a fallback. One: the thirty-day snapshot represents the real trend — so in week one I pull ninety days and validate every diagnosis in 1:1s before acting. Two: benchmarks from comparable verticals apply here — that's why every number is labeled a hypothesis and the week-four checkpoint exists to catch drift early. Three: open entry can generate five hundred sign-ups — I hedge with agency pre-commitments, twenty to thirty percent outreach over-provision, and a paid boost trigger at week four. Four: the fan second-creation loop transfers into this vertical — it's deliberately off the critical path, and there's a fallback if it doesn't fire. The mechanisms survive wrong assumptions; only the numbers move."

### A4. 你考虑过哪些其他方案?为什么没选?

**考点**:决策痕迹。guideline 原题,之前零准备。⚠️
**框架**:四个被否方案,每个一句否决理由。

**中文要点**
1. **五个人平均给资源** → 稀释。数据明确说该分层:Echo 每小时 $318,Vinyl $62,平均主义等于把钱从高回报挪到低回报
2. **只做线上赛,不做线下** → 放弃了音乐节独有的资产:真实主舞台的故事性("从房间到主舞台")和现场内容工厂。线上赛 TikTok 常年有,不稀缺
3. **买量拉观众** → 贵、留不住、不解决供给端问题。这个组合缺的是新观众进来后"有东西可看",供给不修,买来的流量会漏光
4. **只推头部做大场面** → showcase 好看,但 8 周后什么都没留下。招募和教育才是长期供给,所以预算集中在赛事+舞台而不是纯营销

**English**

> "Four options I rejected. Spreading resources evenly across all five — the data says revenue per hour spans five x, so equal treatment moves money from high return to low return. Running online-only — that throws away the festival's unique asset, a real main stage, which is the whole story: from your room to the main stage. Buying traffic — expensive, doesn't stick, and doesn't fix the supply side, which is the actual constraint. And a pure top-creator showcase — it looks great for a weekend and leaves nothing behind. That's why the budget concentrates on the competition and the stage: they're the only pieces that compound."

### A5. 再给你两周,你会做什么?

**考点**:知道自己方案的边界。guideline 原题。
**框架**:数据 → 人 → 风险 → 执行,四件事。

**中文要点**
1. 拿内部数据把 baseline 和 ROI 校准,搭增量归因的口径(见 C3)
2. 访谈五位创作者:数据只告诉我 what,1:1 才有 why——Nova 的下滑是内容疲劳还是时段问题,数据看不出来
3. 把版权清单真正跑一遍(最大风险,现在只有流程没有清单)
4. 把八周计划细化到 runbook:每周谁交什么、卡住了找谁

**English**

> "Four things. First, replace every estimated number with internal data — recalibrate the baseline and build the incremental attribution model for ROI. Second, sit down with the five creators. The data tells me what is happening; only the 1:1s tell me why — whether Nova's decline is content fatigue or a scheduling problem changes the fix. Third, actually run the licensing inventory — it's my top risk and right now I have a process, not a cleared list. Fourth, turn the eight-week plan into a runbook: who delivers what each week, and the escalation path when something slips."

### A6. 做这份作业时最大的困难是什么?

**考点**:真实感 + 自我认知。
**框架**:一个真困难 + 我怎么解的 + 一个诚实的残留。

**中文要点**
- 最大困难:只有 5 行数据、30 天窗口,没有基线、没有流量来源、没有行业对照。风险是把快照当趋势
- 解法:把"我需要什么数据"写成 P2 的一节;所有目标标注"假设,W1 校准";诊断只下我能防守的结论
- 诚实残留:ROI 是行业参考推的,不是内部口径算的——所以我把算法全部摊开写在 P2,等着被挑战

**English**

> "The hardest part was drawing defensible conclusions from five rows and thirty days — no baseline, no traffic sources, no benchmark. The risk is mistaking a snapshot for a trend. I handled it three ways: I wrote 'additional information I would request' directly into the deck; I labeled every target as a hypothesis to recalibrate in week one; and I only drew conclusions the table itself can defend. The honest residual is ROI — it's built from industry reference points, not internal conventions, which is why I put the full calculation on page two instead of hiding it."

### A7. ⚠️ 哪些能复用到你要面的垂类(美妆时尚生活方式)?哪些不能?【垂类迁移题,几乎必问】

**考点**:方法迁移能力。case 是音乐,岗位是 A-List。
**框架**:方法全复用 → 机制换皮 → 有一块要重建。

**中文要点**
- **直接复用(方法层)**:诊断框架(rev/hr × MoM × 回访结构)、分层策略(Back/Fix/Hold)、基线纪律、五阶段活动漏斗、DRI 治理、风险预案——全部垂类无关
- **换皮(机制层)**:线下锚点从音乐节换成时装周/品牌大促/美妆节点;二创从"粉丝剪 set"换成试色挑战、GRWM 模仿、变装 transition;"原创作品评审"换成"教程质量/专业度评审"
- **要重建(变现层)**:音乐 LIVE 基本纯打赏;美妆时尚是**打赏 + 电商带货双轨**,指标要加 GMV、转化率、客单价,创作者分层也要按"打赏型/带货型/双栖"重分
- 收口:这正是我想在面试里请教的——A-List 现在打赏和带货的结构是什么样

**English**

> "The methods transfer completely: the diagnosis framework — revenue per hour, momentum, returning-viewer mix — the tiering logic, baseline discipline, the five-phase funnel, the DRI governance. None of that is music-specific. The mechanisms need reskinning: the offline anchor becomes a fashion week or a brand moment instead of a festival; fan second-creation becomes try-on challenges and GRWM formats. What genuinely has to be rebuilt is monetization. Music LIVE runs almost entirely on gifting. Beauty and fashion runs on two tracks — gifting plus live commerce — so the metric set needs GMV, conversion, and basket size, and creator tiering has to distinguish gifting-led from commerce-led creators. That's actually the part I'd most like to learn about the current A-List structure."

## B · 创作者与数据

### B1. 讲一下你分析创作者的思路(P3 怎么做的)

**框架**:先找矛盾 → 建效率指标 → 交叉验证 → 一人一个结论。

**中文要点**
- 起点:表里最刺眼的矛盾——时长和增长反着走
- 建了 rev/hr(效率)、结合 MoM(方向)、returning%(观众结构)三个维度
- 散点图:回访率 × 增长,分出"漏桶型"(新客不留)和"断粮型"(死忠但没新客)
- 每人一个结论 + 证据,直给,不留模糊

**English**

> "I started from the loudest contradiction in the table: hours and growth move in opposite directions. Then I built three lenses — revenue per hour for efficiency, month-over-month for direction, and returning-viewer share for audience structure. Crossing them separates two failure modes: leaky bucket, where new viewers come but don't stick, and discovery-starved, where a loyal core gets no new viewers at all. Every creator gets one verdict with evidence attached — back, fix, or hold — because a diagnosis you can't act on isn't a diagnosis."

### B2. Back/Fix/Hold 的分类依据是什么?

**框架**:效率 × 趋势的二维矩阵 + 结构确认。

**中文要点**
- 两个主轴:每小时营收(效率)+ MoM(趋势);回访结构做确认
- Back = 高效率 + 正增长(Echo $318/+22%,Luna $303/+9%):放大
- Fix = 低效率 + 负增长 + 结构失衡(Nova/Vinyl:最多时长、最陡下滑、81%/87% 回访但没新客):修结构
- Hold = 同样的模式在早期(BeatTheory −7%):低成本干预,防不是救
- 不是拍脑袋分层,每个判断能指回表里的数

**English**

> "It's a two-by-two: efficiency — revenue per hour — against momentum, with returning-viewer share as the structural check. Back means high efficiency and positive momentum: Echo and Luna, so amplify what works. Fix means low efficiency, steep decline, and a broken audience structure: Nova and Vinyl stream the most hours, decline the fastest, and their returning share above eighty percent with no new viewers says the funnel's top has closed. Hold is the same pattern at an early stage — BeatTheory at minus seven percent — where a cheap intervention now prevents an expensive rescue later."

### B3. 如果 Vinyl 拒绝降时长,坚持"播得久才有机会",你怎么说服?

**考点**:创作者管理的真功夫。⚠️ 高频。
**框架**:不争观点 → 她自己的数据 → 收入兜底 → 小试点。

**中文要点**
- 不跟她辩"你播太多了"——先听:126 小时背后可能是收入焦虑,降时长=降收入的恐惧
- 用她自己的数据:126 小时换 $62/小时,同垂类 Echo 38 小时 $318;她的观众 87% 是回头客——播得再久也是同一批人在看
- 关键:降时长不等于降收入,试点期做**收入兜底**(激励对冲前 2–4 周的波动)
- 不要求全改:选 2 周做 A/B,只动她自己数据里最差的时段;数据说话,好了再扩
- 兜底:她实在不动,尊重——先做 Nova,用 Nova 的结果说服她

**English**

> "First I'd listen, because 126 hours usually isn't a strategy — it's income anxiety, and 'stream less' sounds like 'earn less.' Then I'd show her her own numbers: 126 hours at 62 dollars an hour, against 38 hours at 318 in the same vertical, and 87 percent of her audience is the same people returning — the extra hours are being watched by nobody new. The key move is removing the fear: a revenue floor during the transition, so the first weeks of fewer, better hours are cushioned. And I'd make it small — a two-week pilot cutting only her own worst-performing slots, measured against her own baseline. If she still says no, I run the same play with Nova first and let his results do the persuading."

### B4. 实际落地中,创作者不配合怎么办?

**框架**:先分动机,再对症;体系兜底。

**中文要点**
- 不配合 ≠ 一种问题,拆四种:不认同(→数据+小试点)、怕收入掉(→激励兜底)、习惯改不动(→拆小步)、关系没到(→先 1:1 建信任再谈改变)
- 方案在 W1 1:1 里共创,不是下发——参与制定的人才执行
- 体系兜底:10% 备选名单;辅导指标和活动指标分开算,不配合辅导不影响他参加活动的资格

**English**

> "Non-cooperation isn't one problem, so I diagnose which one it is. Disagreement gets data and a small pilot. Income fear gets a revenue floor. Habit gets smaller steps. And if the relationship isn't there yet, no tactic works — the first 1:1s are for trust, not homework. Structurally, plans are co-built in week-one 1:1s rather than handed down, because people execute what they helped design. And the system hedges: a ten percent backup roster, and coaching participation never gates festival eligibility — those stay separate."

### B5. ⚠️ 假设内部数据显示这 5 人流量 80% 来自 FYP 推荐、只有 5% 来自关注——你的策略怎么变?【老师原题,有标准思路】

**框架**:强化 Fix,但改打法:降时长必须配私域沉淀。

**中文要点**
- 这个信息**强化**诊断,但改变动作
- 风险暴露:Nova/Vinyl 极度依赖公域,直接降时长 = 公域曝光暴跌,收入跟着跌
- 策略从"单纯降时长"改为"降时长 + 同步做私域沉淀":每场固定关注引导、开播提醒/订阅、粉丝团——把公域流量转成私域资产
- 指标同步改:加"观看→关注转化率"做先行指标
- 这正说明为什么 P2 要请求流量来源数据——它直接改变动作设计

**English**

> "That data would strengthen the diagnosis but change the play. If eighty percent of their reach is FYP-driven, cutting hours naively would crater their public exposure — the algorithm rewards live time. So the fix becomes two moves in one: reduce hours while aggressively converting public traffic into owned audience — follow prompts as a fixed segment every stream, live reminders, subscriptions, fan community. And I'd add watch-to-follow conversion as a leading indicator. This is exactly why traffic-source data is on my additional-information list — it doesn't change the diagnosis, it changes the mechanics of the fix."

### B6. 为什么 Echo 是 P1 而不是 Luna?

**框架**:边际收益,不是重要性排序。

**中文要点**
- Echo:效率最高($318)、增长最快(+22%)、但回访率最低(48%)——上升期 + 一个明确短板 = 辅导边际收益最大
- Luna:每一列都均衡(60% 回访、$303、+9%)——接近自身最优,动作是把她的打法提炼成 playbook 给全组用,不是改她
- P1 是"我的时间先花在哪",不是"谁更重要"

**English**

> "It's a marginal-return call, not a ranking of importance. Echo has the highest revenue per hour and the fastest growth, but the lowest returning-viewer share at 48 percent — a rising creator with one clear gap, which is exactly where coaching pays back most. Luna is balanced on every column; she's near her own ceiling, so the move is codifying her playbook for the whole portfolio, not changing her. P1 means my hours go to Echo first — it doesn't mean Luna matters less."

## C · 预算与 ROI

### C1. 预算分配的逻辑是什么?

**框架**:集中在能复利的 → 节奏 40/40/20 → 老师定的 50/50 口径。

**中文要点**
- $28K 舞台+制作、$18K 奖励与差旅 = 57% 集中在赛事+舞台,因为它们复利:赛事产生内容,舞台产生故事,营销只是放大它们
- $16K 营销、$8K 训练营、$4K 现场、$6K 应急(8%)
- 节奏:约 40% 预热 / 40% 活动期 / 20% 长尾——留 20% 给赛后,因为 D30 留存是核心目标
- 创作者向支出五五开:一半给头部+高潜,一半给招募

**English**

> "Concentration on what compounds. Stage plus competition take 57 percent, because they generate the assets — the competition creates content and stories, the stage creates the payoff moment, and marketing amplifies what they produce rather than replacing it. Pacing is roughly forty percent warm-up, forty campaign window, twenty tail — I deliberately protect the tail because day-30 retention is a headline goal, and most campaigns starve it. Within creator-directed spend, it splits half to top and high-potential creators, half to recruitment."

### C2. 50% 给头部,和你的招募目标冲突吗?

**框架**:头部是招募的诱饵,不是竞争者。

**中文要点**
- 不冲突——头部就是招募漏斗的一部分:新人报名的理由是"能上主舞台、能被头部带"(mentor/co-stream/showcase)
- 招募本身的钱在教育、营销、机构外联里
- 头部产出的内容(切片、对决、决赛)是最便宜的获客素材,直接降招募成本

**English**

> "They're the same funnel, not competing budgets. New musicians sign up because of what the top creators make visible — the main stage, the mentorship, the co-streams. That's the aspirational pull; recruitment spend proper sits in education, marketing, and agency outreach. And the content the top creators generate — clips, duels, the finale — is the cheapest acquisition material we have. Backing the top is how the recruitment funnel gets its magnet."

### C3. ⚠️ 你的 ROI 三块相加有重复计算(Double Counting)——老创作者的增长里可能包含新人引流的贡献。怎么拆?【老师点名:方案最大漏洞】

**框架**:承认 → 这正是保守报 5× 的原因 → W1 建增量归因。

**中文要点**
- 直接承认:对,现在是粗略估算,三块之间有重叠风险,这是这份估算最大的弱点
- 这也是我报 5× 不报 6× 的原因之一:参考区间 5–6×,我取保守端
- W1 建增量归因:①组合提升用每人自己的基线对照;②新人收入独立记账(新账号,天然干净);③参与创作者的提升找**非参与对照组**(同垂类、未参赛、可比规模),条件允许用倾向匹配(PSM)选对照
- 重叠部分原则:保守归属,只算一次
- 收口:估算的作用是论证量级成立(≈5×),不是精确到小数点;精确归因是 W1 的活

**English**

> "You're right, and I'd own that directly: the three components overlap, and that's the weakest part of the estimate. It's one reason I report five x, the conservative end of the five-to-six reference range. In week one I'd build proper incremental attribution: portfolio uplift measured against each creator's own baseline; new-creator revenue tracked independently, which is naturally clean since the accounts are new; and participating-creator uplift read against a matched control group of comparable non-participating creators — propensity matching if the pool allows it. Any overlap gets attributed conservatively, once. The estimate's job was to show the order of magnitude holds; precise attribution is a week-one job with real data."

### C4. $400K 的增量收入是怎么算出来的?

**框架**:三块拆开报,每块给逻辑。

**中文要点**
- 组合提升 ≈$70K:现有组合月营收 $62.1K,按目标 +12–15% × 活动窗口
- 参与创作者提升 ≈$250K:头部翻倍目标(+100%)× 参赛的现有及新晋头部
- 新人收入 ≈$80K:500 报名 → 150 首播 → 按新人平均产出爬坡估
- 全部是行业可比区间取保守中位,W1 校准

**English**

> "Three components. Portfolio uplift, about seventy thousand: the current portfolio runs 62 K a month, and the target is twelve to fifteen percent over the campaign window. Participating-creator uplift, about two hundred fifty: that's the plus-100-percent target applied to the creators who compete, existing and emerging. New-creator revenue, about eighty: five hundred entries, a hundred fifty first-time streamers, ramping at conservative per-creator averages. Every input is a comparable-vertical range taken at the conservative midpoint, and all of it gets recalibrated in week one."

### C5. ⚠️ 预算砍半($40K),目标不变,你砍哪块?【老师原题,有标准思路】

**框架**:保推流质量和创作者奖励,砍线下体验和付费营销。

**中文要点**
- 原则:创作者只关心两件事——"推流质量"和"能不能火"。保这两个
- **保**:赛事奖励(报名的动力)、直播推流质量(showcase 的底线)
- **砍**:线下规模降级(booth 缩小、装饰/伴手礼全砍)、付费营销转 organic(靠创作者自传播+平台位)、bootcamp 转纯线上
- Stretch 全部放弃
- 500 报名目标不变,但达成路径从"营销拉"变成"机制拉"(奖励梯 + 头部带)

**English**

> "The principle: creators care about two things — stream quality and a real shot at breaking out. Everything that serves those stays; everything else gets cut. So I keep the competition prizes, because that's why people enter, and the streaming production quality, because the showcase is the product. I cut the offline experience down to minimum — smaller booth, no decoration, no merch — move marketing to organic, creator-driven distribution, and take the bootcamp fully online. Stretch items all go. The 500-entry target stays, but the path shifts from paid amplification to mechanism pull: the reward ladder and the top creators' reach."

### C6. 500 报名、150 首播,怎么实现?

**框架**:渠道拆解 + 超配 + 检查点。

**中文要点**
- 渠道:①站内已标记的音乐内容创作者池(数万级,短视频任务低门槛入口);②机构/Creator Network 预承诺;③现有五人向各自观众推;④外联超配 20–30%
- 150 首播 = Rising 组的设计目标:短视频任务算参赛,首播是晋级动作,门槛一级一级抬
- W4 检查点:报名不达线触发付费投放 + 机构追加

**English**

> "Channel by channel. The tagged music-creator pool on platform is tens of thousands, and short-video missions give them an entry point below the usual go-live barrier. Agencies and creator networks pre-commit volume before launch. The five managed creators recruit from their own audiences. And outreach is over-provisioned twenty to thirty percent because conversion never hits plan. The 150 first-time streamers is what the Rising division is engineered for — entering costs a short video, advancing requires going live, so the ladder pulls people up one step at a time. If entries lag at week four, that triggers the paid boost and another agency push."

## D · 活动与落地风险

### D1. 粉丝二创没人剪,或剪出来质量差,Plan B?

**框架**:机制上不靠自然发生 → 官方兜底 → 预算可转移。

**中文要点**
- 设计上就没赌"自然发生":1v1 邀请制、给模板和素材包、按赞数分层给奖励
- 质量差:现场剪辑台(P7)是官方兜底——官方切片保量保质,粉丝二创是放大器不是唯一来源
- 完全不起量:这块预算和运营人力转投创作者互推/co-stream 内容,同样服务传播目标
- 它不在关键路径上——赛事和舞台不依赖二创成立

**English**

> "The design never bets on it happening organically: it's invited one-on-one, with templates and asset packs, and rewards tiered by traction. If quality is poor, the official clipping desk is the floor — we produce the highlight supply ourselves, and fan clips are an amplifier, not the source. If it simply doesn't fire, that budget and effort move to creator co-streams, which serve the same distribution goal. And it's deliberately off the critical path — the competition and the stage don't depend on it."

### D2. W1 发现头部创作者最火的翻唱曲目全拿不到授权,怎么办?

**框架**:早发现是设计目的 → 三条替代路 → 底线规则。

**中文要点**
- W1 清权就是为了这个——8 周前发现是可解问题,第 7 周发现是事故
- 三条路:①转原创(评审机制本来就偏原创作品,semifinal 看 original sets);②平台已清权曲库白名单内选替代曲目;③和 label 谈活动打包授权——label 有曝光动机,决赛舞台是他们想要的场景
- 底线不动:未清权的作品不上台

**English**

> "That's exactly why licensing clears in week one — found at week one it's a solvable problem, found at week seven it's an incident. Three routes: lean into originals, which the judging already rewards — semifinals are judged on original sets; substitute from the platform's pre-cleared catalog; or negotiate a campaign license package with the labels, who have their own incentive — a festival main stage is exposure they want. The hard rule doesn't move: no uncleared set reaches the stage."

### D3. 音乐节结束后数据回落怎么办?【老师给了思路:驻场+巡演季】

**框架**:设计里就有长尾 → 加两个绑定机制 → 目标是新基线不是保峰值。

**中文要点**
- 回落是必然的,目标从来不是保峰值,是**留下更高的基线**:D30 ≥40%、组合 MoM 转正
- deck 里已有:30 天孵化期覆盖每个参赛者、20% 预算留给长尾、recap 内容延烧
- 再加两个绑定机制(老师思路):①决赛得主绑定为**季度驻场创作者**——固定栏目、平台资源持续;②W9 立即启动**巡演/连麦季**——决赛选手互访直播间,热度接力
- 校准预期:活动的真正产出是资产(新创作者、playbook、内容库、合作关系),不是那两周的峰值

**English**

> "The decay is expected — the goal was never to hold the peak, it's to land on a higher baseline: day-30 retention above forty percent and portfolio momentum positive. The deck already builds for the tail: a thirty-day incubation program for every entrant, twenty percent of budget reserved for post-festival, and recap content extending the story. I'd add two binding mechanisms: the winners become quarterly resident creators with a fixed program slot, and week nine launches a co-stream season — finalists touring each other's rooms so the heat hands off instead of dying. The real outputs are assets: new creators, a playbook, a content library, and partner relationships."

### D4. ⚠️ W4 检查点:数据说不达标,BD 说赞助商已签必须办——你听谁的?【老师原题,跨部门冲突】

**框架**:拆穿伪二选一 → gate 管加码不管取消 → 数据给两边用。

**中文要点**
- 先拆题:这不是"办不办"的二选一。W4 gate 管的是**招募线要不要加码**,不是活动取消键——赞助承诺照常履行
- 数据不达标触发的是补救:付费投放、机构追加、必要时下修目标并**主动同步**所有干系人(包括赞助商侧的预期管理)
- 对 BD:给他看的不是"数据不行",是"缺口多大 + 补救计划 + 对赞助权益的实际影响"——赞助商买的是现场+内容曝光,报名数缺口未必伤他的权益
- 本质:预设决策规则(pre-agreed decision rule)的意义就是把这种争论从 W4 提前到 W1——立项会上就该对齐"如果不达标怎么办"

**English**

> "First I'd reject the framing — it's not go versus cancel. The week-four gate governs whether recruitment needs reinforcement, not whether the festival happens; the sponsor commitment gets delivered either way. Below-target entries trigger the remediation plan: paid boost, another agency push, and if needed a revised target communicated proactively to every stakeholder, sponsors included. With BD, I don't bring 'the data says no' — I bring the size of the gap, the recovery plan, and the actual impact on sponsor deliverables, which are mostly on-site and content exposure, not entry counts. And the deeper answer: this is why the decision rule was pre-agreed at kickoff. The whole point of a gate is that we argued about this in week one, not week four."

### D5. Product 排不上定制礼物/特效,Final Drop 还成立吗?

**框架**:Core/Stretch 已分层 → 运营替代 → 情绪点保住。

**中文要点**
- 定制特效在 Stretch,方案设计时就没把它放进 Core 的承诺里
- Final Drop 的情绪核心是"冠军之歌当晚被全平台送出去"——用现有礼物 + 限定头像框 + 官方置顶推荐同样能成立,产品定制是放大器
- 和 Product 的相处方式:W1 就提需求进排期,W3 确认 scope(P8 里写了"product scope confirmed"),排不上走 fallback,不临时抱佛脚

**English**

> "Custom effects sit in the stretch plan precisely because product bandwidth isn't mine to promise. The emotional core of the Final Drop is the winner's own track being celebrated across the platform that night — existing gifts, a limited avatar frame, and official featured placement deliver that; the custom gift amplifies it. And the working rhythm matters: the request goes into product's queue in week one, scope gets confirmed by week three — it's in the plan — so if it doesn't land, we're executing a fallback we chose, not improvising one."
