## 一、图文三连环(主打 · 垂类经营 + 创作者成长)

**核心论点**:我擅长通过「定标准 → 1v1 诊断 → 规模化复制」的组合拳,系统性提升一个内容垂类的供给质量和创作者活跃。

### 中文骨架(老师模板)

- **职责**:own 抖音图文若干内容垂类的内容供给和创作者增长
- **背景**:图文是新形态,资源有限;垂类多、不知道该投哪里;"有用又好看"的质量要求没法执行
- **第一步 · 精准选垂类**:建三因子框架(规模 × 增速 × 图文相对视频的优势),选中摄影、艺术——图文真正打得过视频的地方
- **第二步 · 标准可执行化**:把"有用又好看"拆成四维质量评分,自己标注内容做验证,让审核和算法都能用
- **第三步 · 头部共创 + 规模化**:按标准选每垂类约 20 个头部,1v1 诊断卡点;把共性卡点变成可规模化项目——主题活动、社群、头部案例下沉给腰部
- **结果**:垂类播放 +7%、优质创作者发布频次 +10%(对大盘基线);框架成为团队标准工具
- **沉淀**:《垂类选择框架》+《四维质量标准》+「1v1 是信息采集不是服务模式」的方法论

### English(90 秒,已有稿微调)

> "At Douyin's photo-and-text team, I owned several content verticals. My job was content supply and creator growth. I did three things. First, prioritization: resources were limited, so I built a three-factor framework to decide which verticals to invest in — we picked photography and art, because that's where photo posts actually beat video. Second, standards: our quality bar was 'useful and beautiful,' but a reviewer or an algorithm can't act on four words, so I turned it into a four-dimension quality rubric and validated it by labeling content myself. Third, creator growth: I used the standard to select about twenty top creators per vertical and coached them one-on-one — diagnosed what blocked each of them, then turned the common blockers into scalable programs: themed campaigns, community channels, and top-creator case studies shared down to mid-tier creators. The results: vertical views grew seven percent, and posting frequency of quality creators grew ten percent, both against the overall baseline. The framework became the team's standard tool."

**30 秒压缩**

> "I owned content verticals at Douyin: built the framework to pick which verticals to invest in, turned a vague quality bar into a four-dimension rubric, coached twenty top creators per vertical one-on-one, and scaled the common blockers into programs. Vertical views grew seven percent against baseline."

**与 A-List 的连接(追问时用)**

> "The structure maps directly: A-List manages top creators in beauty, fashion and lifestyle — my verticals included exactly these aesthetic categories, and the play is the same: define what good looks like for the vertical, diagnose each top creator individually, then productize what you learn."

**可能的追问**
- "1v1 怎么规模化?" → 已有 30 秒稿:1v1 是采集信息,20 个人找出 2–3 个共性卡点,做成项目覆盖几千人;+10% 是全组口径
- "四维标准是哪四维?" → 内容质量(原创/互动/分享回流)、深度专业度、视觉完成度、垂类适配——对齐通用的"内容 × 影响力 × 商业力"作者分逻辑
- "为什么选摄影艺术不选美妆?" → 诚实答:按"图文相对视频优势"选,美妆强在视频演示;**但美妆的图文种草(教程图、成分卡)当时也是重要供给,我运营的头部里就有美妆向作者**(如实际不符则删)⚠️ 核对后再用

## 二、44k 规模化(主打 · 规模化 + 商业化 + 美区实感)

**核心论点**:我擅长用「标准 → 分层 → 自动化」把达人运营从人肉服务变成系统,把人力集中到头部和例外。

### 中文骨架

- **职责**:美区 agency,own 44,000 达人数据库的运营体系与品牌×达人匹配效率
- **背景**:达人量大、AM 人力少(一人只能服务一个品牌);合作一次性、不复购
- **第一步 · 诊断**:用 GMV 和内容表现数据找出真正驱动业务的达人——头部 20% 贡献绝大部分产出
- **第二步 · 标准与分层**:按 GMV 贡献 × 内容表现分层,不同层给不同资源;策略先小跑(工具先给几个 AM 试,再铺 300+ 品牌)
- **第三步 · 系统化 + 匹配引擎**:CRM 自动分层自动触达;自建推荐引擎做品牌×达人匹配,把一次性合作变成复购
- **结果**:AM 人效从 1 个品牌到 2–3 个;我完整经历了美区 TikTok Shop Black Friday / Cyber Monday
- **沉淀**:分层 SOP + 匹配逻辑;「人力只花在头部和例外」

### English(75 秒,已有稿微调)

> "One sentence first: standards, segmentation, and automation — human effort goes only to top creators and exceptions. At a US agency I owned a forty-four-thousand-creator database. Four steps. Diagnose: use GMV and performance data to find which creators actually drive the business. Standards: segment by GMV contribution and content performance, each tier gets a different resource level. Pilot: every strategy starts small — our planning tool went to a few account managers first, then rolled out across three hundred plus brands. Systemize: the CRM does tiering and outreach automatically. And the real lever is matching efficiency: I built a recommendation engine to match the right creator to the right brand, turning one-time collaborations into repeat ones. The direct result: an account manager used to handle one brand; now they handle two to three. And I worked through TikTok Shop's Black Friday on the US side, hands-on."

**30 秒压缩**

> "Forty-four thousand creators, too few account managers. Standards, segmentation, automation: data-driven tiering, a CRM that runs itself, and a recommendation engine matching creators to brands. Account managers went from handling one brand to two or three."

**与 A-List 的连接**

> "Beauty and fashion is the most brand-dense vertical on the platform — top creators there earn through brand work and live commerce as much as gifting. Matching efficiency between brands and creators is exactly the lever I built, and I've run it in the US market, through a US shopping season."

**可能的追问**
- "达人分层的标准?" → GMV 贡献 × 内容表现;对齐通用作者分(内容 × 影响力 × 商业力)
- "推荐引擎怎么做的?" → 品类/画像/价格带/历史转化匹配 + 复购信号;先规则后迭代 ⚠️ 按真实实现口径说,别过度包装
- "这和 LIVE 有什么关系?" → 带货直播就是这个体系的下游场景;美区大促我在机构侧完整跑过流量分配和转化链路

## 三、拍转编(主打 · 数据驱动 + 跨团队 + Ownership)

**核心论点**:我擅长用漏斗诊断找到系统里真正的瓶颈,并用数据说服多个团队改规则。

### 中文骨架

- **职责**:TikTok 特效团队实习,编辑页特效供给 P0 项目 owner
- **背景**:编辑页特效需求约是供给的 10 倍,美区用户测试永远是旧特效;人人看得见问题,没人解释得了原因
- **第一步 · 拆漏斗**:提交 → 过滤 → 测试 → 人审 → 上线五层,逐层量通过率,倒推顶部需要多少量
- **第二步 · SQL 挖池子**:发现真正的问题——一条过滤规则把热门特效误杀了,尤其是美颜和滤镜类(在编辑场景完全可用)
- **第三步 · 改规则 + 建分发**:重设过滤逻辑,推动新测试阈值和更多审核产能(四个团队改规则),设计 Trending/New 标签页分发逻辑
- **结果**:日均提交量约 12 倍,编辑页每天有新的热门特效
- **沉淀**:「漏斗诊断 + 误杀分析」可复制到任何内容供给链路——包括 LIVE 创作者供给(注册 → 开播 → 留存 → 优质化)

### English(90 秒,已有稿)

> "Let me share an example from TikTok's effects team, where I used data to change how our whole review pipeline worked. TikTok effects have two entry points: the camera page and the editing page. I was the owner of a P0 project to grow the supply of editing effects. The starting point was a problem everyone could see but no one could explain: demand for editing effects was almost ten times the supply, and US test users always saw the same old effects. So I did two things. First, I broke down the pipeline — submission, filtering, testing, human review, launch — measured the pass rate at every layer, and worked backwards to calculate how much volume we needed at the top. Second, I dug into the content pool with SQL and found the real problem: one filtering rule was killing popular effects by mistake — especially beauty and filter effects, which worked perfectly well for editing. So I redesigned the filtering logic, pushed for new testing thresholds and more review capacity, and designed the distribution logic for the Trending and New tabs. Daily submissions grew about twelve times, and the editing page finally had fresh, popular effects every day."

**30 秒压缩**(已有稿)

> "Demand for editing effects was about ten times the supply, and no one could explain why. I broke the pipeline into five layers, measured pass rates at each one, and found a filtering rule that was killing popular effects by mistake. I redesigned the logic and got four teams to change the rules. Daily submissions grew about twelve times."

**与 A-List 的连接**

> "Two connections. The funnel method transfers to creator supply directly: sign-up, first stream, retention, quality — same diagnosis. And the effects that were being killed by mistake were beauty and filter effects — the exact content this vertical's users create with."

**可能的追问**
- "你一个实习生怎么推动四个团队?" → 不是我说服的,是数据说服的:每一层的通过率摆出来,误杀案例摆出来,改规则就从"求人帮忙"变成"共同止损"
- "如果重来一次?" → 更早把各团队拉进诊断过程——我最初自己闷头算了两周,拉人进来后推进速度快得多

## 跨团队"对齐难搞干系人"专用版(guideline 明确考点,大概率 Zora 问)

> 用拍转编,但重心从"数据赢了"改成"**理解对方 → 让他也赢**"。

**中文要点**
- 分歧:我要改过滤规则,规则 owner 团队反对——规则是他们防低质内容的防线,改了出问题算他们的
- 我怎么判断:他们不是反对我,是在守自己的指标。所以不争对错,先搞清他们怕什么
- 动作:①拿误杀数据给他们看(热门+合规的内容被挡);②方案改成**分场景阈值**——拍摄页维持原规则,编辑页放宽,他们的防线没有破;③提议灰度上线+回滚机制,风险兜底写清楚
- 结果:规则改了,他们的质量指标没有掉,后来这个分场景逻辑成了他们自己的标准做法

**English(75 秒)**

> "The hardest alignment was with the team that owned the filtering rule I wanted to change. To me the rule was killing good content; to them it was their defense line against low-quality effects — if they loosened it and something slipped through, that was their metric on the line. So I stopped arguing about who was right and worked out what they were protecting. Three moves. I showed them the false-kill data: popular, fully compliant effects being blocked. I reshaped the proposal so their defense held — keep the strict rule on the camera page, relax it only for the editing scenario. And I proposed a gradual rollout with a rollback trigger, so the downside was capped and owned jointly. The rule changed, their quality metrics held, and that scenario-based logic later became their own standard practice. What I took away: a difficult stakeholder is usually just someone whose risk you haven't priced in yet."
