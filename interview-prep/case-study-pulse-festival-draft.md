## 核心叙事(一句话)
**"The portfolio isn't underworking — it's inefficient. Fix it with archetype-based coaching, and use Pulse Festival as the discovery and recruitment engine."**
(组合的问题不是不努力,是效率;festival 不只是活动,是给存量解 discovery、给增量开漏斗的引擎——两条线拧成一个故事。)

## 数据洞察(Slide 2 的底层计算)

| Creator | LIVE hrs | ACV | Revenue | $/hr | MoM | Returning | 诊断 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Echo Music | 38 | 1,250 | $12,100 | ~$318 | +22% | 48% | 漏桶:新流量涌入留不住 |
| Luna Live | 58 | 910 | $17,600 | ~$303 | +9% | 60% | 健康标杆 |
| BeatTheory | 90 | 310 | $10,400 | ~$116 | -7% | 75% | 缓慢下滑 |
| DJ Nova | 115 | 430 | $14,200 | ~$123 | -18% | 81% | 回音室:忠核无新客 |
| Vinyl Nights | 126 | 185 | $7,800 | ~$62 | -20% | 87% | 重度回音室+过度开播 |

三个可讲的规律:
1. **时长与增长负相关**:播最多的跌最狠(Vinyl 126h/-20%),播最少的涨最快(Echo 38h/+22%)→ 问题不是努力,是效率
2. **两种病两套药**:高回访+下跌 = **discovery 饥饿**(忠实小圈子,没有新观众进来);低回访+上涨 = **retention 漏损**(算法给了新流量,接不住)
3. **$/hr 跨度 5 倍**($62–$318)→ rev/LIVE-hour 应该成为组合的核心效率指标(北极星候选)

## 逐页草案(8 页,英文标题定稿,正文要点)

### Slide 1 — Title + Executive Summary
**Title**: "Pulse Music Festival x TikTok LIVE: My First Month" + 副标 approach 一句话
**Exec summary 三条**(每条一行):
1. Diagnose first: the portfolio's problem is efficiency, not effort — coach by archetype, not by template
2. Use Pulse as an engine, not an event: a competition funnel that recruits new creators and gives existing ones the discovery they're starved of
3. Measure supply first: revenue follows retention and discovery, so leading metrics gate every dollar of the $80k

### Slide 2 — Portfolio Diagnosis
**Action title**: "The most active creators are declining: this is an efficiency problem, not an effort problem"
- 上面的表(加 $/hr 列)+ 一张 2x2:x=MoM growth, y=returning viewers → 四象限点名两种病
- 两个 archetype 命名:**Discovery-starved loyalists**(Nova, Vinyl)/ **Leaky-bucket risers**(Echo)
- 一句话:"87% returning viewers with -20% revenue isn't loyalty — it's a ceiling."

### Slide 3 — Creator Strategy: Prioritization & Coaching
**Action title**: "Prioritize where coaching changes the trajectory: grow two, fix two, sustain one"
- **Grow**: Echo(第一优先:势头是易逝品,趁热修 retention——固定档期、fan club/subscriptions、welcome ritual、把 surge 转粉)+ Luna(轻扶持:提炼她的 playbook 复制给全组合,适度加时长)
- **Fix**: Vinyl(最尖锐:$62/hr,减时长提质量、换内容形态、co-host 引流)+ Nova(内容刷新 + 错峰实验 + festival 曝光注入新客)
- **Sustain**: BeatTheory(群体 coaching,监控)
- **Additional info I'd request**(必答小节):traffic source split(FYP vs followers)、new vs returning 绝对量、礼物集中度(whale 依赖)、subscription 渗透、时段分布、>30 天历史趋势、观众转粉率
- **Success metrics**:rev/LIVE-hour(组合北极星)、discovery 组的新观众占比、Echo 的回访率、60 天内 MoM 转正

### Slide 4 — Festival Activation Overview
**Action title**: "One festival, three funnels: recruit the new, energize the existing, showcase the top"
- 四个 partnership 目标 → 三条 funnel 收拢(recruit / energize / showcase,excitement 是三条的合成结果)
- **$80k 预算分配表**(带理由):
  - On-site LIVE stage & booth(舞台/设备/串流/人力):$28k
  - "Road to Pulse" competition(奖池+决赛选手差旅):$18k
  - Marketing & community(co-branded 内容、话题、投放):$16k
  - Creator bootcamp(制作+小额激励):$8k
  - On-site ops & misc:$4k;Contingency ~8%:$6k
- 注:平台流量/产品资源按 in-kind 假设,不占现金预算(标明 assumption)

### Slide 5 — Activation: Recruitment & Education
**Action title**: "Recruit through competition, retain through education"
- **"Road to Pulse" 赛事漏斗**(拉新主引擎):面向站内音乐/DJ 创作者开放报名(含未播过的短视频音乐人——先查存量!),周榜晋级,评审分×礼物票混合排名,Top 3 登 Pulse 真实舞台
  - 引用公开先例:"TikTok has run this format before (On Deck: Breakaway) — I'd adapt the proven mechanics"(调研信号)
  - 激励绑质量:纯礼物票会诱导刷量 → 评审分做门槛(风险页呼应)
- **4 周 LIVE bootcamp**(教育):开播基础、变现阶梯(1k 粉 Gifts→Subscriptions→Rewards)、retention rituals;新老创作者混编,组合五人当导师(showcase 顺便解他们的 discovery)
- 赛后承接:新创作者进 30 天孵化追踪——festival 不是终点,是 pipeline 起点

### Slide 6 — Activation: On-site, Marketing & Partners
**Action title**: "On site, the festival is both a stage and a content factory"
- **On-site**:TikTok LIVE 舞台(决赛+组合头部表演直播)、艺人后台 co-stream、观众互动 booth(现场注册开播引导)
- **Marketing/community**:co-branded 话题挑战、每周赛事高光短视频反哺 LIVE、KOL 联动
- **External partner management**:festival 主办方(deliverables + SLA + 周会)、agencies/Creator Networks(报名渠道下单——机构最会动员)、音乐厂牌(艺人 co-stream 授权)
- 一句个人背书(口头 present 时说,slide 不写):运营过 5,000+ creator 线下活动的 check-in 与分层

### Slide 7 — 8-Week Project Plan
**Action title**: "Eight weeks, five milestones, one weekly heartbeat"
- 时间线:W1 诊断+kickoff+指标对齐 → W2 competition 上线+bootcamp C1 → W3-4 周榜赛+coaching 落地+营销起量【M:W4 报名数 go/no-go 检查点】→ W5-6 半决赛+on-site 细节锁定(舞台/技术/法务)【M:logistics locked】→ W7 决赛选手公布+彩排+技术演练 → W8 festival 执行【M:showtime】→ W+4 汇报:新创作者 30 天留存(pipeline 验收)
- **Stakeholders**:内部 Product/Data/Marketing/Legal&Policy/Ops;外部 festival 主办、agencies、厂牌
- **Keep on track**:weekly standup + owner/deadline 表 + 实时 dashboard + 预算 burn 追踪
- **Biggest risks**(表格:风险→缓解):①报名不足→机构预承诺+W4 检查点触发投放加码;②现场技术故障→冗余串流+W7 全流程彩排(capacity math 教训的正面应用);③礼物票诱导低质→评审分门槛;④主办方交付延误→SLA+周会;⑤预算超支→8% contingency+周度 burn

### Slide 8 — Success Metrics & First-Month Report
**Action title**: "Measure supply first — revenue follows"
- 三层指标表:
  - **Leading(供给)**:新音乐创作者首播数、competition 报名数、组合质量加权开播时长、bootcamp 完课率
  - **Process(参与)**:campaign 期音乐垂类观看时长、组合新观众占比、ACV
  - **Lagging(变现)**:组合 revenue MoM 转正、rev/LIVE-hour、新创作者 D30 留存
- 金句:"Supply and retention are the cause; revenue is the effect. I act on leading metrics and get judged by lagging ones."
- 收尾一行:What leadership sees at Day 30 —— diagnosis done, coaching live, competition running, festival on track
