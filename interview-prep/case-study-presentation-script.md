# Pulse Case Deck — Present 讲稿脉络(2026-08-04)

> 用法:三幕故事,每页第一句和过渡句背到一字不差,中间内容允许 20% 现场变体。总时长 ~17 分钟,每页讲完停一秒再说过渡句。

## 大脉络钩子(开场 30 秒的立论)
"你们给了我两个任务——救组合、办音乐节。别人当两件事做,我的判断是:这是一件事。组合缺的是新观众(discovery),音乐节就是一台 discovery 机器。"

## 三幕结构
- 第一幕 为什么(S1-S3):立论 → 数据证据(两种病)→ 处方。赢得"会看数据、敢取舍"的信任。
- 第二幕 怎么干(S4-S6):预算集中逻辑 → 赛事引擎 → 地面执行。
- 第三幕 怎么算赢(S7-S8):里程碑与风险 → 指标哲学收尾。

## 逐页(第一句 + 要点 + 过渡)

### S1 立论(1 min)
第一句:"Thanks for the time. Before I walk through the slides, here's my one-line answer: the portfolio's problem is efficiency, and the festival is the engine that fixes it. Everything else is detail."
三卡各一句带过,不展开。
过渡:"Let me show you why I framed it this way, starting with the data."

### S2 诊断(3 min,全场锚点,讲最慢)
第一句:"The first thing I did was compute a column that wasn't in the data: revenue per LIVE hour. It spans five times across the portfolio, from $62 to $318."
散点引入:"And when you plot growth against returning viewers, the portfolio splits into two diseases."
Echo:"new viewers are flooding in, but only 48% come back — a leaky bucket."
Vinyl:"Vinyl Nights streams the most hours, keeps 87% of viewers, and is declining fastest. That's not loyalty, that's a ceiling — a loyal echo chamber with no new viewers."
金句:"So the portfolio doesn't need more hours. It needs better hours."
过渡:"Two diseases, two treatments. Here's who gets what."

### S3 Creator strategy(3 min)
第一句:"My priority order is not by revenue — it's by where coaching changes the trajectory: grow two, fix two, sustain one."
Echo 优先理由:"momentum is perishable — a surge unconverted this month is gone next month."
主动念 additional data 栏(显示知道 30 天数据局限)。
过渡(两幕焊接句,最重要):"Coaching fixes efficiency. But coaching cannot manufacture new audiences — that's what the festival is for."

### S4 预算与三漏斗(2 min)
第一句:"I read the four partnership goals as three funnels: recruit the new, energize the existing, showcase the top — excitement is the output of all three, not a separate workstream."
预算逻辑:"I put 57% of the budget on two assets — the stage and the competition — because they compound: the competition creates the story, the stage broadcasts it, and marketing only amplifies what already exists."
过渡:"Let me zoom into the engine itself."

### S5 Road to Pulse(2.5 min)
第一句:"'Road to Pulse' is an eight-week open competition where the prize is real: the top three perform on the actual festival stage."
讲法顺序(页面重构后):①漏斗四格扫一遍(Open entry 强调含未开播音乐人 → Weekly rounds 混合赛制 → In Sync 机制半决赛 → Finals);②奖励阶梯:"This is On Deck's reward design: career capital over cash. The top of the ladder isn't bought, it's platform access — a stage slot, distribution support, your own track as a limited Gift. That's why eighteen thousand covers it.";③房间内机制:"And inside every entrant's room run the same production mechanics I watched live in On Deck rooms: an exclusive campaign gift, a milestone progress bar, a live rank badge, and a shout-out ritual.";④混合赛制理由(如被问):"wherever an incentive points, distortion appears right next to it";⑤底条收尾:"the festival is a pipeline, not a finish line."
先例段(S5 底部的 proven-mechanics 条,展开讲 20 秒):"None of these mechanics are invented. The prize ladder mirrors On Deck: Breakaway, distribution prizes mirror Music On Stage, co-hosting uses In Sync, and gift-integrated tracks come from Songs of LIVE. I'm assembling validated parts, not gambling the budget on new ones."
一手细节(评审记住你的时刻,视时间选用):"I actually tried to sign up for On Deck: Breakaway myself, and the eligibility gate blocked me. That told me something: the official campaigns serve established streamers. The entry-level runway is the gap — and that's exactly the gap Road to Pulse opens, by letting musicians who have never gone LIVE enter."
过渡:"That's the eight weeks before. Here's what happens on the ground."

### S6 现场+伙伴(1.5 min,讲快)
第一句:"On site, the festival is both a stage and a content factory."
厂牌:"licensing is the long pole, so clearances start week one."
个人背书:"I've run on-site operations for a five-thousand-creator event, so I know exactly where these things break."
过渡:"All of this only works if it ships. Here's how I keep it on rails."

### S7 计划与风险(2.5 min)
第一句:"Eight weeks, five milestones, one weekly heartbeat."
重点:W4 checkpoint——"if entries are behind target at week four, contingency triggers a paid boost — I don't wait until week seven to find out."
风险表主动讲。
过渡:"Finally — how do we know we won?"

### S8 指标收尾(2 min)
第一句:"I measure supply first, because revenue follows."
金句:"Supply and retention are the cause; revenue is the effect. I act on leading metrics, and I'm happy to be judged by lagging ones."
收尾:"That's my first month: diagnosis done, coaching live, competition running, festival on track. Thank you — happy to go deeper anywhere."

## 追问预案
1. Vinyl 减时长收入更低? → "His marginal hour earns $62 against a portfolio average around $180 — the audience is exhausted, not growing. Fewer hours at higher quality, plus co-host slots and festival exposure, attacks the real constraint: discovery. If new-viewer share doesn't move in four weeks, I revisit."
2. $28k 舞台太贵? → 唯一同时服务 showcase+内容工厂+品牌现场感的资产;固定成本必须先锁,竞赛/营销可随 W4 调。
3. 报名不够? → 风险表第一行:机构预承诺 + W4 触发投放。"The plan fails loudly at week four, not quietly at week eight."
4. 没办过音乐节? → 承认 + 5,000 人活动实操 + "what I don't know — stage production — is exactly what the festival partner is for; that's why the SLA and weekly syncs are in the plan."

- ▸ 停顿代替 like;说错就地修正不倒回句首
- 语速降 20%,尤其 S2 的数字段
